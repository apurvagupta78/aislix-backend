# Aislix — Product Positioning & USP

## Core promise

**From Retail Image to Verified Action.**

Aislix converts a retail photograph into structured execution intelligence — not just a product count.

## Workflow differentiation

| Step | What Aislix does |
|------|------------------|
| **See** | Detect products, brands, facings, placement from the image |
| **Score** | Retail Execution Score from configured metrics only |
| **Prioritize** | Next-best actions ranked by severity and business rules |
| **Act** | Assign fixes to the right role (field, store, category, executive) |
| **Verify** | Rescan after fix — before/after score and OOS comparison |
| **Learn** | Track recurring issues when historical data exists |

## What we do NOT claim

Individual capabilities (SKU recognition, planogram compliance, share of shelf, OOS detection) are established in the market. Aislix differentiates on the **combined closed-loop workflow**, not on being the only vendor for any single feature.

## Data integrity principle

Metrics show one of: **available · calculated · estimated · not configured · insufficient evidence**.

Never fabricate planogram scores, competitor share, price compliance, or revenue impact without configured data.

## Three separate confidence concepts

1. **AI confidence** — model recognition certainty  
2. **Recognition coverage** — % of facings mapped to known SKUs  
3. **Retail Execution Score** — physical retail performance (excludes model confidence)

## Role families (one product, four views)

| Role family | Hero question |
|-------------|---------------|
| Field executive | What needs attention? |
| Store / operations | How is my store performing? |
| Category / brand | How is my brand/category performing? |
| Business / regional | Where should I intervene? |

## Competitive context (research summary)

Platforms like Trax, Pensa, and Focal Retail excel at image recognition and planogram compliance in enterprise deployments. Aislix targets mid-market and growth retailers with:

- Faster time-to-value (photo → actionable list in minutes)
- Role-aware presentation without separate apps per job title
- Fix → rescan → verify loop built into the product experience
- Transparent metric states (no fake planogram or revenue numbers)
